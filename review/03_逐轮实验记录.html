The low Macro-F1 is driven by the near-zero recall of Acetaldehyde (Class 3) on validation data. By applying a higher loss weight to Class 3 samples, the model will be penalized more heavily for misclassifying them, thereby improving recall for this class and the overall Macro-F1 score.
模型裁决原文
The candidate artifact demonstrates a substantial improvement in the target metric, Macro F1, on the local public validation split. The parent model achieved a Macro F1 of 0.7140, while the candidate achieved 0.9229. This improvement is driven by the successful resolution of the primary failure mode identified in the hypothesis: the near-zero recall for the Acetaldehyde class (Class 3). In the parent model, Acetaldehyde recall was 0.0 (0/109 correct), with most samples misclassified as Ethanol. In the candidate model, Acetaldehyde recall increased to 0.9725 (106/109 correct). Although this shift resulted in a decrease in recall for the Ethanol class (from 0.9634 to 0.8232) and a slight dip in Acetone recall (from 0.9925 to 0.9831), the net effect on the macro-averaged F1 score is strongly positive. The confusion matrix confirms that the model is now correctly identifying the previously missed Acetaldehyde samples, with only minor leakage into Ethanol and Acetone. Given that the task contract specifies maximizing Macro F1 and the candidate significantly outperforms the parent on this metric within the authorized local validation scope, the candidate is the superior artifact.
